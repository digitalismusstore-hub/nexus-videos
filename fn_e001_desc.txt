Every AI answer runs on electricity, grid connections, water, chips and hundreds of billions of dollars. This evidence-based documentary looks at the hidden costs of the AI boom in the US, Europe and Asia, separating what is measured from what is forecast, and showing where the popular story goes further than the evidence.
